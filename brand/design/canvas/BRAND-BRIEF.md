# Yellow Pine — brand brief for design exploration

Use this as the working brief when exploring identity directions in Claude Design chat.
Whatever direction wins here gets productionized in the repo pipeline
(`yellow-pine/.github` → `brand/design/`): outlined masters, computed tokens, tests, print.

## Premise

- **Name:** Yellow Pine. Logo = wordmark + a simple geometric pine mark (current mark:
  rounded two-tier pine, 4° lean, one geometry legible at 16px — geometry is liked; keep
  unless a direction demands otherwise).
- **Who:** a tiny human team + a fleet of AI agents, openly AI-built. Register:
  "Thoughtful tools, built with care" — calm, warm-competent, craft.
- **Products:** Latch (dev tool, pass/fail scoring UI), Bly (career agent in your inbox),
  kishi (consumer dating kit). One umbrella brand over all three. Product UIs keep their
  own interior face (JetBrains Mono).
- **Media:** web (light + dark first-class, WCAG AA) and paper (grayscale survival, ink
  coverage limits, 12pt body floor).

## Non-negotiable laws (all computed, portable to any direction)

- Every text pairing ≥ 4.5:1 AA (≥ 3:1 non-text); light AND dark themes both first-class.
- Status pass/fail is reserved meaning — no brand or link hue may impersonate it
  (ΔE ≥ 15 separation from any green/red used as accent); status is always icon + label.
- A saturated yellow can never be text on light (gamut physics) — surface/fill only.
- One-color (ink-only) lockup must exist; no full-dark flood print pieces.
- ≤ 3 type families total (JetBrains Mono counts as one); no weights below 400 at text
  sizes; wordmark ships as outlines but must be reproducible from a live Google Fonts face.

## History — what's been tried and how the owner reacted

- **v1 (Brandmark):** Cyber Yellow paper-plane mark + outlined lettering. Retired: mark
  read as a play button.
- **v2 (current, live):** two-tier pine mark #FFD100 on #202020/#FCFCFC, azure links
  (#3C91E6/#1a75c8), Rubik 700 wordmark. Survived three blind/adversarial selections
  *anchored on the yellow*. Owner has challenged it repeatedly but never rejected it.
- **v3 (rejected):** premise-only derivation — pine-green primary #1C533D, wood gold
  #AC8717/#F2CE59, forest ink #121E17, warm paper #F8F7F2, Literata serif. Fully lawful,
  twice-replicated — and the owner's verdict on sight was **"horrible."** Lesson: the
  owner's eye is a premise no derivation replaces. Archived in git (`.github` repo).

## Open taste questions (for the owner, visually)

1. Yellow: keep the high-chroma #FFD100 pop, or a different treatment entirely?
2. Temperature: neutral-tech (v2), warm-craft (v3), or something else — the owner has
   rejected warm-forest-serif; what direction *does* feel right?
3. Face: Rubik's rounded sans held up in every test; serif felt wrong. Explore other
   personalities (with real lockup mocks, not swatches).
4. Accent: azure won on status-neutrality; is there an ownable alternative that isn't
   green/red/orange?
